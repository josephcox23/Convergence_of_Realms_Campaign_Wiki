# Aethelgard Empire
* **Summary:** A displaced human kingdom from another plane driven by industrial survival, military authoritarianism, and heavy mining of magitek crystals.
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * Emperor Raythor Drax: Supreme sovereign burdened by an apocalyptic secret.
  * Crown Prince Valen Drax: Supreme commander of defensive lines.
  * Princess Lyra Drax: Chief technomancer and industrial coordinator.

# Elven Sovereignty
* **Summary:** The proud, native indigenous people of this plane, currently fractured by royal infighting, succession crises, and the loss of their ancestral capital.
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * Varion Althil: The fragile king sitting on a tense, newly unified throne.
  * Silar Althil: Hardened commander of the border legions advocating total war.
  * Elenai Althil: Arch-druid managing the deep woods and fighting industrial pollution.

# Core-Drive Syndicate
* **Summary:** A ruthless mega-corp operating on industrial pragmatism, aiming to monopolize all regional energy veins and automate the planet.
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * Director Kaelen Voss: Regional head of extractions.
  * Commander Vespera Cross: Head of corporate security and enforcement.

# Mycelial Concordat
* **Summary:** Radical bio-purists and eco-terrorists fighting to eradicate all synthetic and technological infrastructure to let the primordial biome reclaim the surface.
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * Matriarch Yvaine Thorne: Voice of the spore-root conclave.
  * Spore-Speaker Malakor: Master of deep rot and plague-spores.

# Iron-Socket Cartel
* **Summary:** Independent underworld scrappers and neutral opportunists who profit off the continental war by trading salvaged battlefield tech and black-market goods.
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * *Local Cartel Brokers & Smuggler Bosses* (Operating out of lawless border zones and neutral safe-havens).

# Fringe Villagers
* **Summary:** A resilient community of frontier farmers and locals deeply tied to the land, currently trying to survive corporate displacement and build a long-term resistance.
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * *Village Elders & Frontier Guides* (Managing secret relocations into deep forest sanctuaries).

# Underground
* **Summary:** A loose, clandestine network of black-market merchants, smugglers, and sympathetic data-hackers operating inside corporate-controlled industrial zones.
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * *Underground Informants & Tech-Smugglers* (Supplying restricted magical materials and contraband).

# Crag-Oath Clans
* **Summary:** Independent northern goliath mountain clans who value strict self-reliance, athletic competition, and unbreakable honor, while sending mercenaries south to trade.
* **Notable NPCs:**
  * High Thane Torvald Stone-Grip: Speaker of the Hearth-Moot and master of the Winter Crucible.
  * Astrid Frost-Veil: Mistress of the border posts and trade negotiator.
  * Bramir Stone-Strider: Legendary captain of the Cinder-Wardens mercenary company.
