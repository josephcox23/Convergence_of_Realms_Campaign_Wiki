# The Long Night

> [!quote] Elven Lamentation
> *"We survived the breaking of the skies, only to tear ourselves apart in the dark. For eight centuries, the stars were hidden by the smoke of our own burning homes. The Long Night did not end because peace was found—it ended because we ran out of graves."*

**The Long Night** is the historical name given to the brutal, multi-generational civil war fought within **Elven Sovereignty**. Spanning nearly eight centuries, this dark era of bloodshed, plague, famine, and despair decimated the elven civilization following **The [[Convergence of Realms/NPCs and Factions/Factions & Lore/History/Convergence\|Convergence]]**. 

The conflict finally concluded just two years ago when **Varion Althil** assumed the throne, though the wounds of the war remain entirely unhealed.

---

## The Catalyst & The Toll

Immediately following the death of the powerful wizard **King Galindor Althil III** and the subsequent planar tearing of the world, a vacuum of power shattered the elven court. The late king's seven heirs **The Children of Althil** refused to cooperate, each claiming absolute right to the eternal throne. 

Because **Sealed Elven Castle** was encapsulated in unbreakable crystal on the day of the Convergence, none of the heirs could physically claim the throne room. Driven by pride and paranoia, the siblings retreated to their respective regional strongholds and mobilized their houses into a total, catastrophic war.

### The True Cost:

* **The Population Loss:** Historical scholars estimate that over 40% of the entire elven population was lost during this conflict.
* **The Dark Awakening:** The name "The Long Night" reflects more than just martial combat. The sheer volume of death, combined with the chaotic magical energy left behind by the Convergence, brought widespread plagues, magical diseases, and unnatural despair that choked the continent. 
* **Other Dangers:** The massive, unburied battlefields of The Long Night acted as a literal dinner bell for the invasive monsters stranded here by the [[Convergence of Realms/NPCs and Factions/Factions & Lore/History/Convergence\|Convergence]], leading to an explosion of necrophages, cursed beasts, and wilderness horrors that still plague the land today.

---

## The Bloody Conclusion

The war ground to a fragile halt two years ago through a combination of exhaustion and political assassination known as [[The Night of Broken Mithral\|The Night of Broken Mithral]]. 

**Varion Althil** managed to execute a series of swift tactical maneuvers, capturing or forcibly absorbing the military remnants of his squabbling brothers and sisters. He declared himself the High King of the Sovereignty, forcing a tenuous armistice.

### The Fractured Reality:

Though Varion technically sits at the head of the command structure, it is a hollow victory. The armies he absorbed are packed with troops and ranking officers who remember the centuries of slaughter. Their loyalty to Varion is completely superficial; many are actively looking for an excuse or a bribe to betray the crown and reignite the civil war for their original royal patrons.
