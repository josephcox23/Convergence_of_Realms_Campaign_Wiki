# The Convergence

The Convergence is the defining cataclysmic event of history. One thousand years ago, the fabric of the multiverse tore open. In a matter of hours, multiple entirely different planes of existence slammed together and permanently fused into this single world. It violently forced together alien mountain ranges, deep-sea trenches, magical beasts, and completely separate civilizations who had never known each other existed.

> [!quote] The Common Legend
> *"For a millennium, the mortal races lived in perfect balance with nature. Then, the skies shattered. The Human Empire fell from the heavens, bringing industry, smoke, and steel. The ancient High Elf King triggered the cataclysm out of madness or spite, dying the exact second the planes collided. Since that day, humanity has acted out of pure colonial greed, mining the planet's lifeblood."*
